Patio Services

Your Backyard, Done Right

Texas homeowners spend real time outdoors — and a well-built patio increases your usable living space and adds measurable value to your home. Red Hammer designs and builds custom patios from scratch, whether you're starting with bare dirt or replacing an existing slab that's cracked and settled.

We handle everything from the grading and base prep through final finishing — concrete pours, decorative stamping, pavers, and outdoor kitchen rough-ins. Every project is built for the local climate, with proper drainage so your investment holds up year after year.
