Countertop Services

Stone That Fits Perfectly

A countertop is one of the highest-use surfaces in your home. It needs to look good and hold up to daily life. Red Hammer handles the full process — from selecting the slab and templating the space to fabrication and final installation — so the fit is exact and the finish is right.

We work with granite, quartz, quartzite, and marble, and install in kitchens, bathrooms, and outdoor kitchen spaces across the DFW and Houston areas. Whether you're upgrading as part of a full remodel or replacing countertops on their own, we make it clean and straightforward.
