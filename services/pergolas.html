Pergola Services

Structure & Style, Built to Last

A well-built pergola does more than look good — it extends your usable outdoor living space and adds real property value. Red Hammer builds custom pergolas from the post footings up, sized and designed for your specific yard and the Texas climate.

We use materials rated for Texas weather — UV-resistant wood treatments, powder-coated steel, or low-maintenance aluminum — and we set every post in properly poured concrete footings so the structure stays level and solid for years.

✓
Custom-sized pergolas designed for your space
✓
Cedar, pine, aluminum, and steel frame options
✓

✓
Proper concrete footing installation
✓
Shade sail, lattice, and louvered roof options
✓
