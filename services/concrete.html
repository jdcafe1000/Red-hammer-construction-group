Concrete Services

Solid Work, Done Once

Texas soil is notoriously tough on concrete. Expansive clay shifts with moisture, and poorly prepared pours crack, heave, and settle within a few years. Red Hammer's concrete crews understand how to prepare, reinforce, and pour slabs that actually last in this climate.

From driveways and patios to shop slabs and foundation work, we approach every pour the same way — proper subgrade prep, correct mix design, steel reinforcement where it counts, and professional finishing that controls cracking and drainage.

Driveways — new pours, replacements, and extensions
✓
Walkways, sidewalks, and entryways
✓
Patio slabs and pool decks
✓

✓
Stamped and decorative concrete finishes
✓
Proper grading for drainage and water management
