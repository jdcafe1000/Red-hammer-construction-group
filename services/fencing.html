Fencing Services

Every Type, Done Properly

A fence that leans, sags, or rots within a few years wasn't installed correctly. Post depth, concrete footings, and proper rail attachment matter more than the material you choose. Red Hammer's fencing crews set posts deep, pour concrete correctly, and attach rails and panels the way they're meant to be attached — so your fence stays level and solid for years.

We install wood, iron, vinyl, and chain link across the DFW and Houston areas. Whether you need a full perimeter fence for privacy or a decorative iron fence for curb appeal, we'll show up, do it right, and be done when we said we would.
