Roofing Services

What We Cover

A roof is your home's first line of defense. When it fails — whether from age, storm damage, or poor installation — the damage spreads fast. Red Hammer's roofing crews have installed and repaired hundreds of roofs across the DFW and Houston areas, and we know exactly what proper work looks like.

We handle everything from full replacements on large commercial properties to targeted repairs on residential homes. If your roof took a hit from hail or high winds, we'll give you an honest assessment of what actually needs to be done — not just what's billable.

✓
Full roof replacements — shingles, metal, flat roofs
✓
Storm damage inspection and insurance claim support
✓
Leak detection and targeted repairs
✓
Flashing, ridge cap, and underlayment work
✓
Ventilation and decking inspection
✓
Licensed, insured, and warranty-backed work
