Common Questions

Will applying affect my credit score?

No. Checking your rate through Acorn Finance uses a soft credit pull, which has no impact on your credit score. A hard pull only occurs if you choose to accept a loan offer.

What projects can be financed?

Any service we offer — roofing, remodeling, concrete, patios, pergolas, countertops, gutters, painting, and fencing. If Red Hammer does it, you can finance it.

How much can I finance?

Loan amounts vary by lender and your qualifications. Acorn Finance works with multiple lenders to find options for a wide range of project sizes.

How long does approval take?

Pre-qualification typically takes just a few minutes online. Once you select an offer, final approval and funding can happen the same day or within a few business days depending on the lender.

Do I need to finance through Acorn?

No — financing is optional. You're always welcome to pay directly. But if spreading the cost over time makes the project easier, we're happy to point you to Acorn Finance.
